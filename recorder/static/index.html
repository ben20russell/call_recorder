    Google Meet names

    On Meet calls, a small Chrome extension tells Call Recorder the names on the call and whose tile shows them talking, so transcripts say who said what, and when you leave the call, so the recording stops even when you go straight on to another Meet call. It reads nothing else and sends only to Call Recorder on this Mac.

    1. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
    2. Click Load unpacked, press ⌘⇧G, paste the extension’s folder , press Return, then click Select.
    3. That’s it. During your next Meet call, this shows Connected.
